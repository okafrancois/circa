0.59.2

November 3, 2026

Circa in macOS search

Application metadata now consistently uses Circa, and downloads contain Circa.app. Existing browser data and credentials are preserved.

An older in-place update may retain the previous application filename. Quit the browser and replace that copy with Circa.app from the new disk image to normalize its name.

0.59.0

November 3, 2026

Gmail at a glance

Hover a Gmail Favorite or pinned tab to preview unread mail without switching tabs. Open a message or compose an email from the preview.

The unread indicator can be hidden from the tab context menu. Mail sessions remain separate between profiles; private windows do not show previews. Sign in normally in the same profile.
