## New

- Live Calendar (experimental): hover a Google Calendar Favorite to see today's schedule with real event lengths, and the tile counts down to your next meeting. Before a meeting, a reminder with a Join button (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams) appears under your Favorites, with a chime; choose 2, 5 or 10 minutes before, or never, from the Favorite's menu. When Circa is in the background, a macOS notification appears instead. Turn the sound off in Settings > Meeting reminder sound. It uses your existing Google sign-in in that profile; nothing is stored.
- The sidebar can sit on the right: View > Sidebar on Right or Settings > Appearance. Window controls move with it.
- Show an optional toolbar with back, forward, reload, the page address and your pinned extensions: View > Show Toolbar (Shift-Command-D), or Show as Toolbar from the sidebar address menu. It follows the page tint, and can show the full URL.
- Search Settings with Command-F. Typos are forgiven, and choosing a result takes you straight to the option.
- Search a site straight from the Command Bar with bang shortcuts like !gh, !w or !yt, before or after your query. Your own Site Search shortcuts work too.
- Closing a tab now returns to the tab you used before it. Links open right below their tab, and Back on a link-opened tab with no history closes it and returns to the opener.
- Close a sidebar tab with a middle click; on a pinned tab it removes the pin. Shift-Command-T brings it back.
- Favorites show an unread dot for any web app that puts a count in its page title, not only Gmail. Right-click a Favorite to hide it.
- Leaving Circa while a video plays moves it to picture-in-picture at the window's top-right; switching back to its tab brings it back inline. Hiding or minimising the window does not trigger it.
- The sidebar media card gains mute, a progress bar with seeking and a picture-in-picture button, and works on muted tabs too.

## Fixed

- Ad and tracker blocking no longer changes files inside the app, preserving its security signature after browsing, and works when your account cannot modify the app.
- The Library (Downloads, Media, Spaces, Boosts, Archived Tabs) uses the Space theme's text colour instead of dark text on dark themes, and the download status line keeps the state and size visible.
- Menu commands remain available after typing an accent dead key or using an input method, including keyboard and accessibility activation.
- In WebKit tabs, Back and Forward across History API entries update the address field.
- Removing a profile no longer crashes the browser when no WebKit page was opened.
- Translation checks on restored pages no longer repeatedly open and close the system language service on macOS 27.
- Link summaries are more reliable when the app is busy.

## Known limits

- Live Calendar shows only the primary calendar; regular recurring events show their start time only and have no Join button; meetings you declined still get a reminder.
- The media card cannot control media inside cross-origin frames or audio not attached to the page; per-tab volume, and previous / next on a muted tab, are not available yet.
- Middle-click does not close Favorites or folders.
