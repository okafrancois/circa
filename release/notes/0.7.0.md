## New

- Little windows have a compact address input, toolbar and Space chooser. Hand their page to a Space when you want to keep browsing in the main window.
- Auto-Archive settings offer delay choices and let you apply a change to the current profile or all profiles.

## Fixed

- A download whose filename becomes taken during the transfer is saved with a numbered name instead of losing the file.

## Smaller

- The developer toolbar follows the page's background color.
