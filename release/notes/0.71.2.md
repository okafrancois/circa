---
date: 2026-11-15
title: Security and privacy fixes with less battery use.
---

## Fixed

- Clearing your history also removes the deleted pages from the iCloud sync journal and from the synced copies in iCloud. "All Time" and removing a profile also wipe the engine's own internal history files the next time Circa starts.
- Icons from websites are checked more strictly before they are displayed, so a malicious page can no longer use an unusual image format to reach the system's image decoder.
- Look-alike addresses in the command bar suggestions now show in their real form. An address that imitates another one with foreign letters (for example a Cyrillic "а" in place of a Latin "a") shows as `xn--…`. Normal international addresses such as `münchen.de` still display normally.
- The app is signed with narrower system permissions: the main process now refuses to load code that is not signed by Circa, and only the engine processes that need it keep the exceptions.

## Smaller

- iCloud sync no longer checks in every 5 seconds. It runs when something changes, when iCloud pushes a change, when you come back to Circa, and every 10 minutes as a safety net. This saves battery and disk writes when you are idle. A change made on another Mac can take up to 10 minutes to arrive if its push notification is missed.
- Swiping between Spaces with a textured theme repaints much less, and tabs with a custom symbol icon no longer rebuild the sidebar on every update.
- Deleting history entries is faster on large histories.

## Known limits

- Deleting a time range or a single site does not yet clear the engine's internal history, only Circa's own.
- After this update, the history database cannot be opened by older Circa versions.
