title: Assign a separate browser profile to a Space.
---

## New

- Use Set Profile or Change Profile in a Space's menu to choose its browsing data. A warning explains that accounts, Favorites and extensions may differ before you switch.

## Fixed

- Little windows keep their dark surfaces when opened from a light Space.

## Smaller

- The Space switcher and website permission prompts have more coordinated hover and dismissal transitions.
