## New

- Ask a question from the Command Bar or open the assistant with Command-E. Move a conversation from the Command Bar to the sidebar without sending the question again.
- Choose Codex, Claude or the available on-device model, with supported model and reasoning controls.
- Add context from selected tabs or folders, stream answers, copy text and code, and follow validated citations back to the page.
- The sidebar follows your Space colors, with separate page and assistant cards, light/dark appearance and reduced-motion support.
- AI stays off until enabled in Settings → AI. Page-reading controls and excluded sites apply to each request, including follow-up history.

## Fixed

- Revalidate conversation history after asynchronous page extraction so removing or revoking a source cannot resend earlier page-derived answers.
