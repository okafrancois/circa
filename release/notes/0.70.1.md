## New

- Circa can now offer Touch ID as a passkey option on this Mac: when a site asks to create or use a passkey, choose this Mac and confirm with Touch ID. Security keys and phone sign-in keep working.

## Fixed

- The engine is updated to r23 for this; Chromium stays on 158.0.8127.37.

## Known limits

- Passkeys saved in iCloud Keychain will follow once Apple grants Circa the browser passkey entitlement.
