## New

- Nightly builds: turn on Settings > Advanced > Receive nightly builds to get new features before they reach everyone. Nightlies update automatically like regular releases and may be less stable. Turning the option off keeps your current build until a newer regular release replaces it; Circa never moves you back to an older version.
- The changelog on the website now lists every release since the first one, with its own page and permanent link for each version, a Stable / Nightly filter and an Atom feed you can subscribe to.

## Known limits

- The first nightly build is published after this release; until then the option has nothing new to offer.
