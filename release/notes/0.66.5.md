title: Separate shortcuts for extensions and the AI assistant.
---

## New

- The AI assistant now has its own shortcut, ⌥⌘E, which you can change or remove in Settings › Keyboard Shortcuts. That list now shows every shortcut you can manage, so a conflict never points to a command you can't see.

## Fixed

- ⌘E opens your first pinned extension again; press it repeatedly while holding ⌘ to move to the next one.
