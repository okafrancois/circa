## New

- Circa now imports compatible installed extensions from another browser and supported Chromium browsers into newly imported profiles.
- Restores internal Manifest V3 packages with their original identity, supported enabled or disabled state, and pin order.
- Keeps extensions isolated in their mapped profiles and registers them through Chromium during the import restart.
- Preserves the existing optional passwords and connections step for another browser imports.
- Validation includes importer and recovery tests, the full native suite, Chromium product/smoke builds, and cold/warm Chromium runs with functional extension execution and a public-store package.

## Known limits

- Extension settings, storage, logins and incognito permissions are not transferred. Existing-profile reimports leave extensions unchanged. Manifest V2, built-in, policy-managed, unpacked, missing or invalid packages are skipped. Firefox and Safari extensions are not supported.
