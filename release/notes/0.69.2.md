title: Updates check that sign-ins and installed extensions survive.
---

## Fixed

- Circa also keeps a private copy of cookie databases and extension registration/preferences before a different build first opens existing profiles. Copies stay on your Mac, are limited to three build changes and 30 days, and are never restored automatically.

## Smaller

- Updates now have to prove that site sign-ins and installed extensions survive before a release can be published. The check runs a real signed update from the current public release, then verifies two profiles through restarts and a following update, including extension settings, permissions and stored data.

## Known limits

- This release retains the r22 engine from 0.69.1. It preserves remaining sessions and extensions, but cannot recreate sessions or extension registrations already removed by 0.69.0.
