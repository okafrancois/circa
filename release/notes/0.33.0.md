## New

- First launch guides you through importing browser data, choosing Favorites, iCloud sync, a Space theme and your default browser. Interrupted setup can resume; existing installations skip it.
- Change tab icons and edit a pinned page's saved address from its context menu.
- Export a Space into a folder or turn a folder into a Space from their menus.
- Choose an application icon for the Dock and Finder from the icon chooser in Settings.

## Fixed

- Link-routing rules can be edited inline and deleted immediately. Split dividers show clearer hover feedback and resize panes live.

## Smaller

- Settings, floating panels, interface icons and typography have a refreshed appearance. Space themes gain a Frost sidebar option.
