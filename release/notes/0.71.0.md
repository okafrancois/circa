title: Media keeps its controls when you leave its tab.
---

## New

- When a tab you switched away from is still playing sound, a small player card appears at the bottom of the sidebar with the site icon, previous, play/pause and next. Hover it to see the tab title and a close button: closing the card pauses the media and keeps the tab, and clicking the site icon takes you back to the tab.
- Several players stack into one card and fan out on hover, newest on top.
- A player paused from its page stays with its controls greyed out, and the card follows you across Spaces. Videos that go into automatic Picture in Picture keep using Picture in Picture instead.

## Known limits

- The volume button is shown but not available yet.
