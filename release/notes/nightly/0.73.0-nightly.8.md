## New

- Live Calendar now shows real event lengths, and three minutes before a meeting a reminder appears under your Favorites with a Join button (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams). Join only opens on click.

## Known limits

- Regular recurring events show their start time only and have no Join button.
- Live Calendar shows only the primary calendar.
