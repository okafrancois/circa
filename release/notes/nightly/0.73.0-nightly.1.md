## New

- Close a sidebar tab with a middle click. Shift-Command-T brings it back.
- Favorites now show an unread dot for any web app that puts a count in its page title, not only Gmail. Right-click a Favorite to hide it.
- Search a site straight from the Command Bar with bang shortcuts like !gh, !w or !yt, before or after your query. Your own Site Search shortcuts work too.
- Show an optional toolbar with back, forward, reload and the page address: View > Show Toolbar (Shift-Command-D). Click the address to edit the full URL.

## Known limits

- Middle-click does not close pinned tabs, Favorites or folders yet.
- The toolbar does not show pinned extensions yet.
