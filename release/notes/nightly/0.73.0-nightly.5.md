---
date: 2026-11-15
title: The media card controls muted tabs too.
---

## Fixed

- On a muted tab, the sidebar media card now pauses, plays, seeks and closes the media, and its picture-in-picture button works.

## Known limits

- Media inside cross-origin frames, or audio not attached to the page, cannot be controlled from the card.
- A tab paused before it was muted still loses its card.
- Per-tab volume, and previous / next on a muted tab, are not available yet.
