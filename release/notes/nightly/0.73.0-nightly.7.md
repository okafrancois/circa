## Smaller

- Live Calendar now works with any Google Calendar Favorite (calendar.google.com, /calendar/r…), not only /calendar/u/N/r addresses.

## Known limits

- Live Calendar shows only the primary calendar, displays events as one-hour blocks, and does not offer Join or meeting reminders yet.
