## New

- Meeting reminders now chime, and you choose when they appear (2, 5 or 10 minutes before, or never) from the Calendar Favorite's menu. When Circa is in the background, a macOS notification with a Join button appears instead. Turn the sound off in Settings > Meeting reminder sound.

## Known limits

- Meetings you declined still get a reminder.
