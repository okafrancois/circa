## New

- Live Calendar (experimental): hover a Google Calendar Favorite to see today's schedule, and the tile shows a countdown to your next meeting. It uses your existing Google sign-in in that profile; nothing is stored.

## Known limits

- Only the primary calendar is shown.
- Event end times are not shown yet; events display as one-hour blocks.
- The Favorite must point at a Google Calendar /calendar/u/N/r address for now.
- Joining a meeting and meeting reminders come later.
