## Fixed

- The sidebar downloads list now opens from anywhere on its button, including the badge, and stays open while you move into it to click a file.
- Gmail and Calendar favorite previews stay open while you move into them, so you can scroll, open a message or click + to compose.
- A favorite's title tooltip no longer covers its open preview.
