## New

- Search Settings with Command-F. Typos are forgiven, and choosing a result takes you straight to the option.

## Fixed

- Ad and tracker blocking no longer changes files inside the app, preserving its security signature after browsing.
- Blocking works when Circa was installed by an administrator and your account cannot modify the app.
- Translation checks on restored pages reuse the system language service to avoid repeatedly opening and closing its connections on macOS 27.
