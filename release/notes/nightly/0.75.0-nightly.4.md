## New

- The app icon you choose in Settings › Appearance now stays in the Dock when Circa is not running, as long as Circa is kept in the Dock, and changes there right away when you pick another one.

## Known limits

- Finder keeps showing the default icon: changing it would modify the signed app.
- After updating, the Dock may only pick up the chosen icon after the next login, or after removing and re-adding Circa to the Dock.
