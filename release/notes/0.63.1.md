## New

- Pages now reach the window edges by default. Turn on “Space around pages” in Advanced Settings to restore the previous margins.
- Added “Use dots for Space icons” in Advanced Settings. Turning it off restores your chosen icons.

## Fixed

- Pinned extension icons follow the address field’s hover state and remain visible while their popup is open.
- The fullscreen sidebar button’s hover background aligns with the address field and Favorites.

## Smaller

- Space dots form a compact group, while chosen icons keep their existing spacing.
