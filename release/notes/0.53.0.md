## New

- Hold Shift while hovering an HTTP(S) link to preview its title and description, followed by up to three AI summary points. Ordinary hover does not start this helper.
- Enable **Link preview summaries** in Settings → AI → Helpers and choose a ready Quick model. Releasing Shift, moving away or navigating dismisses the preview and cancels pending work.

## Known limits

- This helper retrieves linked pages anonymously, without browser cookies or authentication. It supports main-frame links to UTF-8 HTML/XHTML up to 256 KiB, with up to four policy-checked redirects; logged-in and JavaScript-rendered page context is not included.
- Site exclusions, private-window restrictions and the existing privacy/provider settings apply. AI remains off by default.
