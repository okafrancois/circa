## New

- Type an extension's name in the Command Bar to run it; its popup opens in a small window, even from a New Tab.
- The sidebar media card's speaker now opens a volume slider for that tab; unmuting restores your previous level.

## Fixed

- Private windows open faster: built-in ad and tracker blocking is now configured inside the engine, without a hidden startup page.
- The built-in blocker no longer appears on the engine's extensions page.
- Live Calendar no longer reminds you about meetings you declined, and hides them from the preview.

## Known limits

- The small extension popup window shows the extension's name in its title bar and its minimise button stays enabled.
- A meeting declined after the last calendar refresh is hidden at the next refresh.
