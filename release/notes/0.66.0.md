Optional tab previews and docked developer tools.

- Tab hover previews are now off by default. Enable “Show tab hover previews” in Advanced Settings; changes apply immediately and your choice is remembered after restarting.
- The fullscreen exit reminder now disappears automatically without leaving fullscreen.
- Picture in Picture’s return-to-tab button selects and brings forward the original page. Closing Picture in Picture keeps the current tab selected.
- Developer tools open docked on the right by default. Their dock menu lets you choose the left, bottom, right, or a separate window.
