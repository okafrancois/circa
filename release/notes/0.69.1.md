## Fixed

- Fixes a Chromium 158 migration bug that signed users out of websites. Circa now preserves both older encrypted cookies and connections created since version 0.69.0 across updates and restarts.

## Known limits

- This update cannot recreate cookies already deleted by version 0.69.0; affected sites may require signing in again once.
