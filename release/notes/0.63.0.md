## New

- Pages now reach the window edges by default. Enable Space around pages in Advanced Settings to restore the previous margins.
- Use dots for Space icons in Advanced Settings replaces Space icons with dots without changing your saved icon choices.

## Fixed

- Pinned extension buttons follow the address field's hover state and remain visible while their popup is open. Navigation clears stale hover state.
