---

## New

- Optional AI tidying shortens newly pinned tab titles and suggests clearer names for completed downloads. Enable **Tidy titles and downloads** in Settings → AI → Helpers, and choose a ready Quick model.
- Hover a generated tab title to restore its original name. Download feedback includes Undo, and the Library follows the file's current name.
- Title suggestions use the known page title; download suggestions use the original filename. File contents are not sent to the model.

## Known limits

- Custom titles and existing pins stay unchanged. Download renaming preserves the extension and file attributes, refuses filename collisions and leaves changed or missing files alone.
- AI remains off by default. The existing page-access, site, private-window and provider settings apply.
