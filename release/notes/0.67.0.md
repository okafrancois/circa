Circa is lighter on your Mac. With iCloud sync on, the browser no longer spends CPU re-checking your whole history every few seconds while you are idle, and typing in a password field no longer keeps a timer running in the background. Tabs you have not looked at for a while are paused until you come back to them, except pages that play audio, are in a call, or can send you notifications. Launch is faster when you use several profiles, because a profile's extensions start only when you open one of its Spaces. Your history database is much smaller after a one-time upgrade on first launch, the session is saved to disk less often, and the sidebar no longer redraws everything when a Favorite or a Split tab changes its title.
