## New

- Hover a Gmail tab to preview unread senders, subjects and message snippets without switching tabs.
- Open a message or compose a new email directly from the preview.
- Show or hide the unread indicator from the tab context menu; the preference is saved separately for each profile.
- Mail sessions and preview state stay isolated between browser profiles. Private windows do not show previews.
- Sign in to Gmail normally in the same profile. Preview availability depends on the account’s inbox feed; unavailable or signed-out feeds do not display cached mail.
