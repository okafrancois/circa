## New

- Create a Boost to change a site's colors, fonts and text size, or use Zap to hide page elements. Find saved Boosts in the Library; they stay separate for each profile.
- Enable Picture in Picture when you leave a video tab in Settings to keep a playing video visible while you browse elsewhere.
