## Smaller

- Long Downloads, Media, History and folder-preview lists reuse the rows and thumbnails near the visible area instead of keeping every item onscreen in memory.
- Background tabs sleep after an hour of inactivity, with their last page image kept visible until restored content paints.
- Startup defers hidden controls, and sync, snapshots and download processing move more work away from the main thread.
