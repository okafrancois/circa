Circa has a new look: space in broad daylight, printed in ink. A crescent-moon icon, new type, paper grain, and a planet for each Space. Planets are the new default Space icon, and emoji and dots are still there. The whole browser follows the new identity, from the first-launch welcome to every Settings pane. The welcome flow now has a progress bar, real site icons when you pin your favourites, and a live preview of your sidebar and first Space. Circa's interface now always stays in its daylight look, even when macOS is in dark mode, while websites still follow your system appearance. Right-click a Favorite or a pinned tab to find Edit Pinned Page: replace its saved address with the page you are on, go back to the saved address, or edit it by hand. These menus also gain Copy Link, Share, Change Icon, Mute, Duplicate and Open in Split View. Space tabs gain Customize Page and Archive Tabs Below.
